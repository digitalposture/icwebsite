**Executive Summary**  
- Both CDs and bonds are low‑risk, fixed‑income investments that pay modest returns.  
- CDs are FDIC‑insured up to $250,000, making them the safest option for short‑term savings【2†L129-L132】.  
- Bonds are uninsured but can offer higher yields; they carry credit and interest‑rate risk【2†L158-L160】.  
- Rising interest rates tend to favor CDs, while bond prices fall when rates climb【2†L133-L134】.  
- Bonds are more liquid than CDs and can be sold before maturity, though possibly at a loss【2†L135-L136】.  

## Safety and Yield Trade‑offs  
Certificates of deposit are bank‑issued time deposits backed by the FDIC, guaranteeing principal and interest up to the insurance limit【2†L152-L154】【2†L169-L172】. Their returns are fixed for the term, which protects investors when market rates fall but can leave them locked into lower rates if rates rise【2†L195-L200】. Bonds, by contrast, are loans to governments or corporations and lack FDIC protection. While high‑quality government bonds are very safe, corporate bonds carry credit risk, and both types are vulnerable to interest‑rate fluctuations that can erode market value before maturity【2†L158-L160】【2†L310-L314】.  

## When to Choose Each Instrument  
CDs work well for investors seeking a predictable, short‑to‑medium‑term place to park cash, especially in a rising‑rate environment where the locked‑in yield outpaces falling‑rate bonds【2†L131-L134】. Bonds suit those willing to accept additional risk for potentially higher income, regular coupon payments, and greater liquidity, making them appropriate for longer‑term goals or diversified fixed‑income portfolios【2†L130-L132】【2†L317-L321】.  

## Portfolio Implications  
A balanced fixed‑income strategy often blends both assets: CDs provide a low‑risk core and emergency‑fund shelter, while bonds add yield and diversification. The optimal mix depends on an investor’s time horizon, risk tolerance, and need for liquidity, and professional advice can help align these choices with overall financial objectives【2†L139-L144】.
