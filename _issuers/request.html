--- title: "Request an Issuer" layout: page permalink: /issuers/request --- If you can't find an ISIN or want iCertificates to track an issuer or a new ISIN, send a request to our AI Agent with the subject 'New ISIN' and include the ISIN(s) and, if you want, the issuer in the body (alternatively, our AI Agent will find it for you)!
Typically, the new ISIN will be available in less than 24hours.
